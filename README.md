# HR Analytics Dashboard: Employee Attrition (Tableau)

An interactive Tableau dashboard that shows how many employees leave, who they are and what they have in common, so HR can focus retention work where it matters.

## Data
1,470 employees (IBM HR attrition dataset) with 39 fields: department, job role, age band, gender, education field, marital status, overtime, monthly income, job satisfaction, work-life balance, years at company, years since last promotion, and attrition (yes/no).

## What the dashboard shows
- Headline KPIs: total employees, attrition count and rate, active employees, average age
- Attrition by department, job role, education field, age band and gender
- Job satisfaction by job role
- Drill-down filters for department and other dimensions

## Tools
Tableau (calculated fields, filters, dashboard actions) · Excel for data preparation
